# NVO987 — Bénévolat

Official volunteer information website:

## About

NVO987 – Culture Visuelle Moderne et Contemporaine is a cultural and
research initiative based in France.

The organization explores modern and contemporary visual culture,
art history, artistic practices, cultural studies, visual perception,
and the relationships between art, research, society, and digital
technologies.

The volunteer website presents opportunities to contribute to cultural
projects, interdisciplinary research, documentation, digital content,
communication, and collaborative activities.

## Current Volunteer Opportunity

### Pédagogue bénévole

The website presents a volunteer opportunity for a pedagogue to support
research activities related to contemporary visual culture.

The opportunity is described as a collaborative, unpaid activity.

Location: Paris, France.

Prospective volunteers should contact the organization for additional
information about the position, eligibility requirements, responsibilities,
and application procedures.
